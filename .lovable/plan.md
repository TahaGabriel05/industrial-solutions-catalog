# Industrial B2B Catalog

## Scope
- Replace the starter screen with a professional industrial catalog website.
- Add separate Home, Products, About Us, and Contact / Quote pages with shared navigation.
- Structure catalog content as Category → Subcategory → Product in reusable data modules.
- Add category cover imagery, product cards, search, category/subcategory filters, and responsive mobile controls.
- Add a quote form that is prefilled from the selected product and collects contact, quantity, and message details.
- Keep the experience catalog-only: no prices, accounts, cart, checkout, or payments.

## Visual direction
- Dark navy, white, steel grey, and restrained safety-orange accents.
- Crisp technical typography, compact industrial layouts, high-quality product imagery, and minimal decoration.
- Desktop sidebar becomes a compact filter drawer/section on smaller screens.

## Technical details
- Use TanStack Router with one route file per page and route-specific metadata.
- Keep multilingual-ready copy and catalog data centralized for future French and Arabic localization.
- Keep quote submission as a validated front-end confirmation because no delivery service or database was requested.
- Verify the catalog, filtering, quote prefill, and responsive layouts in the running preview.
